How do I play?

Drag to move your paddle and flick to smash the ball. Use the slider on the right to bend the field deeper. Don't let the ball get past you, and destroy every invader.

Can I play with one hand?

Yes. Pause the game and choose ONE FINGER. Then drag sideways to move and drag up or down to change the depth.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads.

Tap RESTORE on the title screen while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tap PRIVACY on the title screen (shown in regions where it applies), or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where are the leaderboards?

Tap LEADERBOARDS on the title screen. You need to be signed in to Game Center.
