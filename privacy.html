Goatastrophe: The Last Rally ("the app") is made by Roozbeh Moayyedian ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no sign-up, and I don't run any servers that receive your information. Your settings, high scores and recovered memories are stored only on your device.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboards. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • information about your device and your interactions with the ads

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but they aren't personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are loaded. You can change your choices at any time with the PRIVACY button on the title screen.
  • No ads: the one-time Remove Ads purchase turns all ads off for good.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
